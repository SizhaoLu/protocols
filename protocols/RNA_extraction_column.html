RNA Extraction with Spin Columns

Reagents

  • RNeasy® Mini Kit (Qiagen Cat# 74106).
  • Buffer RLT (add β-mercaptoethanol at 10 µL per 1 mL if working with RNase-rich tissues): 350 µL per sample.
  • 70% ethanol prepared with RNase-free H₂O: 350 µL per sample.
  • Buffer RPE: Add ethanol as directed when opening a new kit.

Note

  • A 6-well plate is usually used for in vitro experiments, with 200,000–300,000 cells per well.

Procedure

  1. Add 350 µL Buffer RLT to the cell culture plate and scrape off the cells.
  2. Transfer the lysate to a QIAshredder column (Qiagen Cat# 79656).
  3. Centrifuge at 20,000 × g for 2 minutes and collect the flow-through.
  4. Transfer the lysate to a gDNA Eliminator column and centrifuge at 8,000 × g for 1 minute. Keep the flow-through in the collection tube and discard the column.
  5. Add 350 µL of 70% ethanol and mix thoroughly by pipetting.
  6. Transfer up to 700 µL of sample to an RNeasy Mini spin column.
  7. Centrifuge at 8,000 × g for 15 seconds. Discard the flow-through.
  8. Add 700 µL Buffer RW1. Centrifuge at 8,000 × g for 15 seconds and discard the flow-through.
  9. Add 500 µL Buffer RPE. Centrifuge at 8,000 × g for 15 seconds and discard the flow-through.
  10. Add another 500 µL Buffer RPE. Centrifuge at 8,000 × g for 2 minutes.
  11. Without disturbing the flow-through, place the column into a new 2 mL collection tube and centrifuge at 20,000 × g for 1 minute to dry the membrane.
  12. Transfer the column to a new 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tube. Add 30 µL RNase-free water directly to the membrane and centrifuge at 8,000 × g for 1 minute.
  13. Collect and label the tubes.
  14. Place RNA on ice while preparing for cDNA synthesis, or store at -20°C.
