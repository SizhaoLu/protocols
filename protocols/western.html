Western blotting

Materials/Equipment

  • Centrifuge cooled to 4℃
  • TissueLyser LT (Qiagen)
  • Sonicator (Branson digital sonifier)
  • BioRad Mini Protein Gel system

Procedures

  1. Protein lysate prep

    • Lysis buffer volume

      Tissue/cell Vol (μL)
      60 mm dish 100-200
      100 mm dish 300-500
      Kidney tissue 15-30 mg* 300-500
      • About 1/5 to 1/10 of a whole kidney
    • For cell culture: scrape cells off the plate in lysis buffer
    • For kidney tissue: use TissueLyser LT
      1. Place 2 ml microcentrifuge tubes containing 1 stainless steel bead (5 mm mean diameter) on dry ice for at least 15 min.
      2. Transfer tissue piece to the tube and incubate on dry ice for 15 min
      3. Put the tube into the TissueLyser Adapter, incubate for 2 min at RT.
      4. Add lysis buffer (with inhibitors added) to the tube.
      5. Operate at 50Hz for 1 min (Add additional 1 min cycle as needed, let the tube rest on ice between cycles)
    • Sonicate the samples at 10% amplitude for 5 sec
    • Centrifuge the lysate at 14,000 RPM (add RCF) for 10 min at 4 ℃

    • Collect supernatant.

      • Measure protein concentration now will making running gel easier.
      • Detergent in RIPA interferes with BradFord, dilute the sample at least 10x for measurement.
      • Bradford Assay is generally performed with 96-well plate (transparent ELISA plates for measuring absorbance)
      • Prepare standard with BSA (dissolve in lysis buffer) ranging from 2 mg/mL to 0.25 mg/mL in 1:10 diluted RIPA.
      • Use 18 uL H2O, 2 uL of lysate/standard plus 180 uL 1x bradford solution.
      • Measure absorbance at 600 nm.
      • Optional but recommended: bring the samples to similar concentrations for more accurate loading

      Keep lysis buffer and lysate cold at all times

  2. SDS-PAGE and Sample Preparation
    • Prepare resolving gel of suitable percentage (see recipe)
    • layer on top with about 1ml of EtOH to make the gel form a sharp line when it polymerizes (takes about 20-30 min).
    • Pour out the ethanol and rinse with milli-Q water. Dry with paper towel.
    • Pour the stacking gel (see recipe) and let it sit at least 30 min before taking the comb out.
  3. If protein amount is not known, Bradford Assay can be done when the gel is polymerizing.
  4. Calculate prepare lysate using excel template. Keep lysate on ice during the preparation
  5. Boil lysate for 5min after adding DTT (1M stock) and sample buffer
  6. During this time, set up the gel using 1x running buffer
  7. Load 8-10 ul of protein ladder.
  8. After samples are loaded, run the gel first at 80 V. After samples reach resolving (bottom) gel part, turn it up to 120 V until done.

Semi-Dry Transfer

  • During the gel run, prepare the transfer buffer.
  • Cut a PVDF membrane out (9.5 cm by 5cm) and notch the right hand corner and write on the right hand corner in pencil (helps remind what side the protein is on). Written side of the membrane will face the SDS-gel.
  • Place membrane in methanol for 5 min. Balance the membrane in 1x transfer buffer.
  • PVDF membrane is hydrophobic. Make sure to soak in methanol first.
  • Cut (match gel and membrane size) and soak two (for each gel) thick filter paper in transfer buffer.
  • Take gel out of apparatus and cut off the stacking. Cut a notch on the top left-hand corner.
  • Balance the gel in 1x transfer buffer for 5-10 min. Careful not to flip the membrane.
  • Assemble the transfer sandwich in the transfer instrument, in the order below (bottom to top).
    • Thick filter paper
    • PVDF (writing facing up, cut and writing on the top right corner)
    • Gel (flip up side down, the cut corner on top right, align with the cut on the membrane)
    • Thick filter paper
  • Roll out bubbles.
  • Cover the lid for the instrument. Transfer at 15v for 30 min. Time can be extended for larger protein.

Blocking and antibody incubations

  • Following transfer, wash the membrane in 1x 10x TBS for 5 to 10 minutes with gentle agitation at room temperature twice.
  • Place membrane carefully in Blocking buffer. Gently agitate the solution and incubate for 1 hour at RT.
  • Incubate with primary antibody (see Antibody dilution information) overnight at 4 °C.
  • 6 mL of diluted antibody is needed if plastic pouch is used.
  • Next morning, wash the membrane four times with TBST for 5 min
  • Second antibody incubation – Prepare secondary antibody solution as follows:
    • Odyssey secondaries can be used for detecting two (or more) proteins on the same blot
