#+TITLE: Immunofluorescence Staining Protocol — OCT (fresh embed) Tissue

* Overview
This protocol describes indirect immunofluorescence staining of
freshly OCT-embedded frozen tissue sections (validated on
mouse kidney).

* Safety notes
- Acetone is volatile and flammable; fix slides in a fume hood or
  well-ventilated area, away from open flame.
- Sudan Black B is a suspected carcinogen and an irritant; handle with
  gloves and prepare/apply in a fume hood. 

* Materials/Equipment
- Humidified chamber
- Coplin jars or slide staining dishes
- Fume hood (for acetone fixation and Sudan Black step)

* Reagents
1. Acetone (-20°C)
2. Phosphate-buffered saline (PBS)
3. Blocking buffer: 1x PBS with 5% goat serum and 2% BSA
4. Sudan Black B (0.05% in 70% ethanol)
   - Stir overnight to fully dissolve.
   - Filter before use to remove precipitate
   - Light-sensitive, store protected from light.
5. DAPI (Invitrogen Cat#D1306)

* Procedures
1. Remove slides from storage (-80°C) and allow to warm to room
   temperature for 5 min.
2. Fix the slides in -20°C acetone (1mL per slide). Incubate for 10
   min, then air dry until the acetone has fully evaporated.
3. Wash the slides twice in 1mL 1x PBS, 5 min each.
4. Block the slides with blocking buffer. Incubate in a humidified
   chamber for 1 hour.
   - Use about 200 μL per kidney section (or 800 μL per slide)
   - Decant the blocking buffer after 1 hour.
5. Without washing, apply diluted primary antibody to the tissue.
   - Use Hydrophobic Barrier Pen to circle the tissue
   - Alternative: cover the tissue with parafilm to prevent evaporations.
   - Incubate in a humidified chamber at 4°C overnight.
   - Protect from light if using conjugated antibodies.
   - Need about 50 μL per mouse kidney section (or 300 μL per slide).
   - [[file:antibody_info.org::#frozen-tissue][Antibody dilution information]]
6. Decant primary antibody solution.
   - If using parafilm method, sink the slide into a tank of DI water
     to allow it to naturally flow and detach without damaging the
     tissue.
7. Wash with 1mL 1x PBS two times, 5 min each.
8. Apply diluted secondary antibody. Incubate 1 hour at room
   temperature in a humidified chamber, protected from light.
   - Secondary antibodies are usually used at 1:500 dilution.500 μL
     per slide.
   - Parafilm not needed for this step due to increased volume.
9. Wash with 1mL 1x PBS three times, 5 min each, protected from light.
10. Optional: DAPI staining
    - The mounting media contains DAPI, but additional staining may
      produce more uniform DAPI signal.
    - Stock solution: 1 mg/mL (2.86 mM) in DI/MQ water.
    - Dilute 2.86 mM stock to 300 μM: 31.47 μL DAPI + 268.53 μL PBS.
    - Dilute the 300 μM intermediate solution 1:1000 in PBS to achieve
      1x working concentration.
    - Incubate for 5 min, protected from light.
    - Wash with 1x PBS twice, 5 min each, before proceeding.
11. Block endogenous autofluorescence with Sudan Black B (1 mL per
    slide). Incubate for 10 minutes at room temperature, protected
    from light.
12. Wash slides twice with DI water, 10 min each.
13. Mount and seal slides.

* Tips and troubleshooting
1. Always include a negative control when possible.
   - Pick the sample expected to have positive signal (i.e., an
     isotype or secondary-only control run alongside a known-positive
     tissue).
2. When applying liquid, avoid pipetting directly onto the tissue, as
   this may cause detachment. Instead, slowly pipet the liquid along
   the side of the tissue section.
