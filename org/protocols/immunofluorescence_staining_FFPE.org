#+TITLE:Immunofluorescence Staining Protocol — FFPE Tissue Sections

* Materials/Equipment
  - Cut paraffin-embedded tissue to 4–6 µm on charged slides.
  - Coverslips
  - Hydrophobic barrier (wax) pen
  - Xylene-resistant slide staining racks/containers
  - Pressure cooker (BioSB)
  - Glass container (for NaBH4 step — do not use plastic)
  - Humidified chamber
  - Nail polish (for coverslip sealing)

* Reagents
  - Xylene
  - Ethanol (100%, 90%, 80%, 70%, 50%)
  - DI H2O
  - PBS (Phosphate-Buffered Saline)
  - PBS-T (PBS + 0.5% Tween-20)
  - Antigen unmasking solution (vector #H-3300)
  - 0.1M Glycine in PBS-T (0.751 g glycine per 100 mL PBS-T)
  - Sudan Black B (Sigma 199664-25G)
  - 5% BSA in PBS-T (2g in 40 mL)
  - Blocking buffer
    1) 8mL BSA (40%)
    2) 8mL Superblock (40%)
    3) 2mL goat serum (10%)
    4) 2ml horse serum (10%)
  - Citrate antigen retrieval solution (or Tris-based alternative; e.g.,
    Vector Antigen Unmasking Solution, #H-3300)
  - Glycine (Sigma G-8898)
  - Sodium borohydride (NaBH4) tablets
  - Clear Hank's Buffer
  - Bovine serum albumin (BSA)
  - Superblock
  - Goat/Horse serum
  - DAPI-containing antifade mountant (e.g., ProLong Gold Antifade with DAPI)

* Procedures
1. Deparaffinization
   | Step   | Time                     |
   |--------+--------------------------|
   | Xylene | 3 min ×3 (fresh changes) |

2. Rehydration
   | Solution  | Time     |
   |-----------+----------|
   | 100% EtOH | 3 min ×2 |
   | 90% EtOH  | 3 min    |
   | 80% EtOH  | 3 min    |
   | 70% EtOH  | 3 min    |
   | 50% EtOH  | 3 min    |
   | DI H2O    | 3 min    |
   | PBS-T     | 3 min x3 |

3. Sudan Black (autofluorescence reduction)
   - 0.1% Sudan Black B in 70% EtOH (0.2 g in 200 mL 70% EtOH) — 20 min, RT
   - Rinse: PBS-T, 3× 3 min

4. Antigen Retrieval
   - Prepare Citrate (or Tris) antigen unmasking working solution,
     1:100 in DI H2O, in clear/glass containers
   - Pressure cooker setup (BioSB)
     1) Fill base of pressure cooker with DI H2O to just cover the bottom of slide containers
     2) Close lid, pressure valve *closed*
     3) Low Pressure [106-110⁰C] 25 minutes
     4) Fully release pressure, remove slides
     5) Cool to touch (10–20 min) before proceeding

5. Glycine Quench (reduce autofluorescence from fixation)
   - 0.1M glycine in PBS-T 
   - 10 min, RT

6. Sodium Borohydride (NaBH4) Treatment
   - 1 tablet NaBH4 dissolved in exactly 100 mL cold, clear Hank's Buffer
   - *Use a glass container, in a fume hood, on ice*
   - Incubate 40 min
   - wash with PBS-T, 3× 3 min

7. Blocking
   - Circle tissue with hydrophobic barrier pen; dry slide carefully
    with a KimWipe (avoid touching tissue)
   - Incubate in Blocking buffer for 2hrs at RT in a humidified
     chamber (check periodically to ensure buffer hasn't evaporated)

8. Primary Antibody
   - Dilute primary antibodies in blocking buffer at optimized concentrations for your targets
   - Incubate overnight at 4°C in a humidified chamber

9. Wash
   - PBS-T, 3–4× 3–5 min

10. Secondary Antibody
    - Dilute secondary antibodies in blocking buffer
    - Incubate 60 min at RT, protected from light

11. Wash
    - PBS-T, 3× 3 min
    - Dry slides carefully (KimWipe around, not on, tissue)

12. Mounting
    - Apply DAPI-containing antifade mountant and coverslip
    - Seal edges with nail polish
    - Allow to dry in the dark
    - Store/image protected from light
