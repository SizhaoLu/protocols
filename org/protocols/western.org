#+OPTIONS: toc:nil
#+TITLE: Western blotting
* Materials/Equipment
  - Centrifuge cooled to 4℃
  - TissueLyser LT (Qiagen)
  - Sonicator (Branson digital sonifier)
  - BioRad Mini Protein Gel system
* Reagents
- RIPA Lysis buffer
| Component        | Stock Conc (mM or %) | Final Conc (mM or %) | Vol (mL) |
|------------------+----------------------+----------------------+----------|
| Tris pH 7.4      |                 1000 |                   50 |      2.5 |
| NaCl             |                 5000 |                  150 |      1.5 |
| IGEPAL CA-630    |                  10% |                   1% |        5 |
| Deoxycholic acid |                   5% |                0.50% |      2.5 |
| SDS              |                  10% |                0.10% |      0.5 |
| H2O              |                      |                      |       38 |
  
#+begin_note
- 5mL Aliquots can be saved at -20 ℃
- Supplement with protease inhibitor and phosphatase inhibitor before use.
#+end_note

* Procedures
1. Protein lysate prep
   - Lysis buffer volume
     | Tissue/cell                | Vol (μL) |
     |----------------------------+----------|
     | 60 mm dish                 |  100-200 |
     | 100 mm dish                |  300-500 |
     | Kidney tissue 15-30 mg^{*} |  300-500 |
     * About 1/5 to 1/10 of a whole kidney
   - For cell culture: scrape cells off the plate in lysis buffer
   - For kidney tissue: use TissueLyser LT
     1) Place 2 ml microcentrifuge tubes containing 1 stainless steel
	bead (5 mm mean diameter) on dry ice for at least 15 min.
     2) Transfer tissue piece to the tube and incubate on dry ice for 15 min
     3) Put the tube into the TissueLyser Adapter, incubate for 2 min at RT.
     4) Add lysis buffer (with inhibitors added) to the tube.
     5) Operate at 50Hz for 1 min (Add additional 1 min cycle as
	needed, let the tube rest on ice between cycles)
   - Sonicate the samples at 10% amplitude for 5 sec
   - Centrifuge the lysate at 14,000 RPM (add RCF) for 10 min at 4 ℃
   - Collect supernatant.
     #+begin_note
     - Measure protein concentration now will following steps easier.
     - Detergent in RIPA interferes with BradFord, dilute the sample at least 10x for measurement. 
     - Bradford Assay is generally performed with 96-well plate
       (transparent ELISA plates for measuring absorbance)
     - Prepare standard with BSA (dissolve in lysis buffer) ranging
       from 2 mg/mL to 0.25 mg/mL in 1:10 diluted RIPA.
     - Use 18 uL H2O, 2 uL of lysate/standard plus 180 uL 1x bradford solution.
     - Measure absorbance at 600 nm.
     - Optional but recommended: bring the samples to similar
       concentrations for more accurate loading
     #+end_note
     #+begin_warning
     Keep lysis buffer and lysate cold at all times
     #+end_warning
2. SDS-PAGE and Sample Preparation
   - Prepare resolving gel of suitable percentage (see [[Polyacrylamide gel][recipe]])
   - layer on top with about 1ml of EtOH to make the interface between
     resolving and stacking gel form a sharp line when it polymerizes
     (takes about 20-30 min).
   - Pour out the ethanol and rinse with milli-Q water. Dry with paper
     towel.
   - Pour the stacking gel (see [[Polyacrylamide gel][recipe]]) and let it sit at least 30 min
     before taking the comb out.
     #+begin_tip
     - If protein amount is not known, Bradford Assay can be done when the gel is polymerizing.
     - If protein is frozen, thaw on ice when preparing gel
     - Standard loading vol for 1.5mm 10-well gel 20 µL
     - Boil lysate for 5min after adding DTT (1M stock) and sample buffer
     - During this time, set up the gel using 1x running buffer
     #+end_tip
   - Load 8-10 ul of protein ladder.
   - After samples are loaded, run the gel first at 80 V. After
     samples reach resolving (bottom) gel part, turn it up to 120 V
     until done.
     #+begin_tip
     - Make sure that the loading dye runs out at the bottom
     - Run gel 
     #+end_tip
3. Semi-Dry Transfer
   - During the gel run, prepare the transfer buffer.
   - Cut a PVDF membrane out (9.5 cm by 5cm) and notch the right hand
     corner and write on the right hand corner in pencil (helps remind
     what side the protein is on). Written side of the membrane will
     face the SDS-gel.
   - Place membrane in methanol for 5 min. Balance the membrane in 1x
     transfer buffer.
     #+begin_note
     - *PVDF membrane is hydrophobic. Make sure to soak in methanol first.*
     #+end_note
   - soak two (for each gel) thick filter paper in transfer buffer.
   - Take gel out of apparatus and cut off the stacking. Cut a notch
     on the top left-hand corner.
   - Balance the gel in 1x transfer buffer for 5-10 min. Careful not to flip the gel.
   - Assemble the transfer sandwich in the transfer instrument, in the order below (bottom to top).
     1. Thick filter paper
     2. PVDF (writing facing up, cut and writing on the top right corner)
     3. Gel (flip up side down, the cut corner on top right, align with the cut on the membrane)
     4. Thick filter paper
   - Roll out bubbles.
   - Cover the lid for the instrument. Transfer at 15v for 30 min. Time can be extended for larger protein.

4. Blocking and antibody incubations
   - Following transfer, wash the membrane in [[10x TBS][1x TBS]] for 5 to 10 minutes with gentle agitation at room temperature twice.
   - Place membrane carefully in [[Blocking buffer]]. Gently agitate the solution and incubate for 1 hour at RT.
   - Incubate with primary antibody (see [[file:antibody_info.org::#western-blot][Antibody dilution information]]) overnight at 4 °C.
     #+begin_note
     - 6 mL of diluted antibody is needed if plastic pouch is used.
     #+end_note

   - Next morning, wash the membrane four times with TBST for 5 min
   - Second antibody incubation – Prepare secondary antibody solution as follows:
   - Odyssey secondaries can be used for detecting two (or more)
     proteins on the same blot
     |      Cat# | Host | Target | Conjugation  | Dilution |
     |-----------+------+--------+--------------+----------|
     | 926-32210 | Goat | Mouse  | IRDye® 800CW | 1:10,000 |
     | 926-68070 | Goat | Mouse  | IRDye® 680RD | 1:5,000  |
     | 926-32211 | Goat | Rabbit | IRDye® 800CW | 1:10,000 |
     | 926-68071 | Goat | Rabbit | IRDye® 680RD | 1:5,000  |

    #+begin_note
    - It is generally recommended that the IRDye 800CW secondary
      antibody (800 nm channel) be used to detect the lower-abundance
      protein target and IRDye 680RD secondary antibody (700nm) to
      detect the more abundant protein
    #+end_note

     - Alternatively, HRP conjugated secondaries are available for chemiluminescence detection.
       | Name                 | Company        | Cat#        | Host   | Dilution |
       |----------------------+----------------+-------------+--------+----------|
       | Goat Anti-Mouse HRP  | jacksonimmuno  | 115-035-003 | Goat   |  1:10000 |
       | Goat Anti-rabbit HRP | Cell Signaling | #7074       | Goat   |   1:2000 |
       | Donkey Anti-Goat HRP | Novex          | A16005      | Donkey |   1:5000 |

     - Incubate at room temperature for 1 hour with agitation. Protect from light
     - Washes
       1) Wash four times with TBST for 5 min
       2) Rinse the membrane with TBS briefly to remove residual Tween 20.
5. Imaging with LI-COR Odyssey XF system.
   - Start with 10 min

* Recipes for gels and buffers

** Polyacrylamide gel

| Reagents                    | Stacking | Resolving 7.5% | Resolving 10% | Resolving 12% |
|------------------------------+----------+-----------------+----------------+----------------|
| 30% Acrylamide/bis (mL)      |      1.3 |               5 |            6.6 |              8 |
| 0.5M Tris-HCl, pH 6.8 (mL)   |      2.5 |               0 |              0 |              0 |
| 1.5M Tris-HCl, pH 8.8 (mL)   |        0 |               5 |              5 |              5 |
| 10% SDS (uL)                 |      100 |             200 |            200 |            200 |
| H2O (mL)                     |      6.1 |             9.8 |            8.2 |            6.8 |
| TEMED (uL)                   |       10 |              20 |             20 |             20 |
| 10% APS (uL)                 |      100 |              64 |             64 |             64 |

#+begin_quote
This calculation is enough for 2 gels with 1.5mm spacer.
10% APS is aliquoted in 200 uL and kept at -20 °C for 6-9 months.
#+end_quote

** 10x running buffer

| Reagents | Concentration | Weight |
|----------+----------------+--------|
| glycine  | 192 mM         | 72g    |
| Tris     | 25 mM          | 15.15g |
| SDS      | 1%             | 5g     |
| H2O      |                | 500 mL |

#+begin_quote
pH of the 10x running buffer should be 8.3 and no pH adjustment is required. Store the running buffer at room temperature and dilute to 1X before use.
#+end_quote

** 10x transfer buffer (Bjerrum Schafer-Nielsen)

| Reagents | Concentration | Weight  |
|----------+----------------+---------|
| glycine  | 39 mM          | 29.3g   |
| Tris     | 48 mM          | 58.2g   |
| H2O      |                | 1000 mL |

#+begin_quote
pH for transfer buffer should be ~9.2. Do NOT add acid or base to adjust pH. For 1x buffer, add 100mL of 10x stock, 200 mL of Methanol to 700 mL of H2O
#+end_quote

** 10x TBS

| Reagents | Weight |
|----------+--------|
| Tris-HCl | 24g    |
| Tris     | 5.6g   |
| NaCl     | 88g    |
| H2O      | to 1L  |

#+begin_quote
PH should be around 7.6 (no need to adjust pH). Add Tween-20 to 0.2% in 1x TBS for TBST
#+end_quote

** Blocking buffer

5% BSA in TBS

** Antibody buffer

Dilute blocking buffer 1:10 in TBST
